# Prompt para Gemini 3.1 Pro — fotos de Aptis Speaking

Cómo usarlo:
1. Abre Gemini y elige **Gemini 3.1 Pro**.
2. Copia y pega **el bloque 1** (las instrucciones generales).
3. Después pega **un bloque de fotos a la vez** (van en grupos de 5) y espera a que termine.
4. Descarga cada imagen y **guárdala con el nombre que Gemini te indique** (por ejemplo `s-009.png`), todas en una carpeta llamada `fotos-speaking`.
5. Cuando tengas las 20, dile a Claude Code: «Las 20 fotos de Speaking están en la carpeta fotos-speaking. Conviértelas a webp de menos de 400 KB y conéctalas según COMO-INTEGRAR-APTIS.md».

Si una foto sale con letras, letreros, logos o una persona rara (manos deformes, caras extrañas), pídele: «Rehaz la foto s-0XX sin texto y con manos y caras naturales».

---

## Bloque 1 — instrucciones (pegar primero)

```
You are going to generate a set of 20 photographs for an English speaking exam practice app (British Council Aptis, Speaking parts 2 and 3: "describe this photo" and "compare two photos").
Rules for EVERY image:
- Photorealistic, like an ordinary candid photo taken with a good phone camera. Natural light. Not artistic, not glossy advertising, no filters.
- Horizontal, 4:3 aspect ratio.
- Absolutely NO text anywhere: no signs, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no brand names, no writing on screens, boards, clothes or packaging.
- No famous people, no recognizable places or landmarks.
- People of varied ages and backgrounds, natural poses and expressions, realistic hands and faces.
- Everyday, neutral scenes a learner can describe: who, where, what they are doing, how they feel.
- One image per description. Under each image write only its file name so I can save it.
Reply "Ready" and wait for the first group of descriptions.
```
